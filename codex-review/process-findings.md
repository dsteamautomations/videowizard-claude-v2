**1. Owner summary**

The remaining risks sit mainly between the approved plan and the people or models making and checking the pictures.
A drawing’s brief leaves out approved material sizes and working methods stored elsewhere in the plan.
The video checker misses the detailed action timetable that the video maker receives.
Other checks oppose a deliberately changing object or a planned camera move.
A clean showroom can still receive instructions to show signs of use.
The drawing rules ask for timing checks that the same instructions say to leave out.
Group approval can pass a picture whose question is still awaiting an answer.
Fix these handoffs and conflicting words first; none needs another production step.
These are findings read from the files, not failures observed in a new film.
