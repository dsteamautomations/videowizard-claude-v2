# design/113 — The drawn-graphic route (design/89 E1–E4, design/112 E1–E5, design/111 F19's labels and view)

Status: requirements for SK's approval, 27 Oct 2026, against `main` at server 0.61.0 (K1–K4 built, not deployed). No code until the questions are answered. Built as its own sub-set after S and K (SK's answer 4 to design/112), with one sample render reviewed by SK before the route becomes the default for technical diagrams.

## What is asked (restated)

A fifth route, **drawn**, for diagrams, charts, maps, timelines and technical drawings. The planner writes a **declarative scene** (never code): a canvas, the film's style sheet, shapes in real units with one scale, and a timeline of actions keyed to a time or a narration word. The server validates the scene and renders the shot's exact frames with Pillow and ffmpeg, in the repo's font (Noto Sans Regular and Bold, OFL; a client's brand font beside it when supplied). The result is reviewed and approved like a take, on the Takes step; re-renders are free and never counted against the cap. The sourced drawing spec (design/89 D, design/106 D1–D6, design/111 F19) is the input of every drawn graphic: the scene's units come from the spec, its known sizes go on the drawing as labels, its view (cutaway, plan, side, elevation) is the drawing's view, and a drawn graphic without an approved spec is refused. The drawn graphic becomes the default route for technical diagrams, charts, maps and timelines; the AI-image graphic sequence stays available, no longer the default for technical diagrams; AI video for graphics stays off.

## What exists

- Graphics today: `visual_type` diagram (one still) or animation (a sequence of 2–4 approved AI states with holds and dissolves, design/65); a style sheet picture per film (design/89 C2) that every graphic prompt binds; claims and the spec's relationships, sizes and label as must lines on the picture check (design/89 C1, D4, D5; design/96 J1, J2); exact text with its place and size (design/111 F13); the spec table approved once per run (design/106 D4); `view`, `view_reason` and `illustrative` on the spec (design/111 F19). The plan check says "or a drawn animation (next release)" at a graphic held over 4 s.
- Rendering: `stage2/render.py` already draws text with Pillow (slates, labels, `_font`) and concatenates pieces with ffmpeg; the handover carries a `media` record per shot (still, sequence, clip, movie).
- Takes step: lists video shots' takes (`take_jobs`), each watched, checked by Gemini and measured in code (design/91 N3, design/112 S5), then selected (`select`), which writes `session['assets'][shot] = {'kind': 'movie', …}`.
- Fonts: none in the repo; `_font` falls back to Pillow's default.
- Word timing: the narration's word frames exist on the plan (design/89 A1, `_tokens`, the "inside the word" placement check); a graphic state already names the `words` it starts on (design/89 C3).

## Unstated but expected

- Old plans load (every new field defaults); a film with no drawn shot renders as today; the AI-image sequence route is untouched.
- One flow, two front ends: the scene is submitted through `vw visuals draw` and through the page (a JSON box on the shot's card, as the spec table is), both calling one server function; approval and rejection are the take's own buttons.
- The style sheet picture stays the look reference of AI graphics; the drawn route reads the style's words (palette, line weight, labels, background) as colours and weights: the planner writes them as hex colours and px weights in `graphic_style` when drawn shots exist (the check asks for them once).
- Deterministic: the same scene renders the same bytes (fixed fonts, no randomness), so a re-render after an unrelated change is a cache hit (digest of scene + size + fps).
- Costs: none per render; stated once on the Takes step ("drawn: free").

## Edge cases and failure modes

- A scene over 300 shapes or 100 actions: refused (a drawing is read in seconds). A text shape under LABEL_MIN_PCT of the frame height, or outside the safe area: refused by the validator, not discovered at render. A word cue the narration of the shot does not contain, or that falls outside the shot's frames: refused with the word and the shot's words. Two actions on one shape overlapping in time: the later wins; a warning. A shape whose dimension is unknown and that carries a figure: refused (design/106 D6). A scene without the spec's known sizes placed: the server adds them (labels on parts); the planner can move a label, never drop it. A font file that Pillow cannot open: the render fails with the file named, Noto is not substituted silently. ffmpeg missing on the VM: the job fails with the message; nothing else is affected (ffmpeg is on the VM today). A render that takes over 60 s (a 4K film, a long shot): the job keeps running under its lease; the page shows "rendering"; no retry storm. A shot re-planned after approval: the asset's digest no longer matches the shot (as a take's does); the status says so. The film's size is 9:16: the canvas keeps its units; the frame fill follows `frame_fill`. Colour: sRGB tagged like the slates (design/46 11.2 reads the tags).

## Questions for SK (answer in order, or say "go with the recommendations")

1. **How much of a drawing language.** E1 lists rect, line, arrow, circle, polygon, text, image, group, repeat and five actions. A wall section, a bond pattern, a chart, a timeline and a map outline fit in that. Recommendation: exactly that set, no curves or 3-D in this release; a drawing that needs more is an AI graphic with a spec until the set grows.
2. **Who places the labels.** Recommendation: the server places the spec's known sizes beside their parts by default (a leader line to the part, the text outside it); the planner may give a position per label; a label the planner drops is put back.
3. **Review model.** Recommendation: one Gemini 3.8 Flash read of the first and the last frame against the claims, relationships and label lines (as the picture check), not the video model's read; the code check covers frames, size, fps, safe area and the sizes-or-label rule. About a quarter of a picture check's cost per drawn graphic.
4. **The sample render for SK.** Recommendation: VW-7's wall-section shot (the rat-trap bond, the spec approved by Revathi), rendered on the VM from a scene I write, before the route is made the default and before the plugin command ships; the sample is posted beside the repo like the live-check pictures.
5. **Brand fonts.** Recommendation: one optional file per film at the brief (TTF or OTF), used by the drawn route and the slates; no font download from the web; Noto Sans in the repo is the default.
6. **The default switch.** Recommendation: the route is the default for technical diagrams, charts, maps and timelines only after SK approves the sample; until then the plan check warns (not refuses) a diagram with exact sizes that is not drawn.

## Tests first

- E1: a scene with any key outside the schema (an executable string, a path, a URL) is refused naming the key; a text shape under the minimum size or outside the safe area is refused; a word cue not in the shot's narration is refused; the spec's known sizes appear as text shapes; an unknown size carries no figure and the label appears.
- E2: a valid scene renders exactly the shot's frames at the film's size and fps (ffprobe); a word-keyed action starts on that word's frame; the same scene renders the same digest twice (cache hit); the 5 % push applies.
- E3: the drawn job appears on the Takes step rows; the code check lines (frames, size, fps, safe area, sizes-or-label) and the Gemini lines ride on the record; approve, reject with words, select as the shot's asset; the handover carries it as a movie; a re-render costs nothing against the cap.
- E4: the font files load from the repo; a brand font file is used when present and its absence is a named failure.
- E5: a drawn shot without an approved spec is refused; the scene's view is the spec's view.
- E6: a diagram with exact sizes not drawn is warned; the treatment and the split count `drawn`; AI video for a graphic stays refused.
- Plugin: `vw visuals draw --shot S --scene file.json` posts the scene; `status` lists the render; the Takes step words.

## Not covered (known before the build)
