# 170 — Wan 3.0 Omni as the take generator: where it enters the flow, what goes, what stays (5 Nov 2026)

SK, 5 Nov evening: the cartoon-guided method (design/153–169) is **parked**, not replaced by another cartoon method. The team tried create.wan.video by hand and the results were very satisfactory, so the visuals stage's AI video takes move to **Wan 3.0 Omni through the Wan CLI**. This note is the requirements step: the exact places Wan enters, the steps that are no longer needed, the steps taken as they are, the missing fourth reference (the environment), the tests before the build, and the build order. Nothing is built until SK approves.

Sources read whole: SK's Claude Doc "Wan CLI Team Setup Guide" (5 Nov, including its code blocks); Wan's own `guidance.md` (install and login); the CLI's `--help` and its bundled `wan-cli` skill (version 0.0.3, installed into this session's scratchpad with scripts off, no key, no account).

## 1. What Wan gives us (facts, not hopes)

| | Wan 3.0 Omni (`wan omni2video`) | Gemini Omni today |
|---|---|---|
| Inputs | up to **10 images**, **5 videos** (≤15 s in total), **5 audio clips** (MP3/WAV, ≤15 s in total), or one brief file / one URL | pictures + text; a video only as an edit or a ≤3 s likeness reference |
| How a reference is named | `@Image1`, `@Image2`, `@Video1`, `@Audio1` in the order passed; a friendly name is written beside it: "the mason (@Image1)" | `Image1` / `Image2` as first and last frame |
| Length of one take | **2–30 s** (`--duration N`), or the model chooses (`--smart-duration=true`) | 3–10 s |
| Sound | generated by default; `--audio-output=false` for a silent take | none |
| Resolution, shape | 480P / 720P / 1080P; `--ratio adaptive, 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 3:4, 9:16` | 720p, the film's shape |
| Output seen | 1280×720, H.264 + AAC, 30 fps, no watermark on Premium | MP4 720p |
| Prompt | up to 20,000 characters | our take text (design/128, timecoded) |
| Cost seen | 30 credits for 10 s at 720P with two images and two audio clips; the price is not shown before submit | about ₹90 a take |
| Time | about 2 minutes a job | 50–110 s |
| Account | one shared key = one pool of credits, **8 video jobs in flight at once**, one history; member-only (error 4018 otherwise) | key pool of our own |
| Calls | `omni2video … --output json` → `taskId`; **`wan result get <taskId> --output json`** polls without blocking; `wan task wait` blocks; `--dry-run` resolves the references without spending | REST submit / poll |
| Also there | `frame2video --first-frame … --last-frame …` (our old start/end way), `videoedit`, `text2video` | |

What the guide and SK's own tests say about its behaviour:
- A reference photograph **passes its pose into the video**: a character photographed with a pan on his head keeps it there unless the text frees his hands, or a neutral-pose picture is sent. Our sets already hold a full-length neutral picture (set picture 1) and a working pose (picture 2): the neutral one is the character reference; the working pose only when the shot wants it.
- Layout and gesture lines are **loose guidance**: a wall came out taller than written; a point at 1 s was not clear. The timecoded take text (design/128) is the right shape for it; SK: "it performs excellently well when we support it with a very good descriptive text of what happens and when it happens".
- Counting: SK's brick test did **a little better than Gemini, without any input video**, with bricks drawn very small. Counts stay out of the text by default (design/165); the test round below measures Wan on it once.
- Smart duration (`--smart-duration=true`): the model ends the take itself. SK: both ways exist; test both.
- 30 s in one take: SK wants the director's play tested inside one take: angles, camera moves, hands.

## 2. Where Wan enters the flow (the glossary's words, design/152)

Visuals is stage 10; its eight procedure steps, and what each becomes:

| Visuals step | Today | With Wan | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Preparation | the package, the parts | the video tools part names Wan's limits (10 pictures, 30 s, the `@Image` names, silent takes) | **changed text only** |
| 2 Treatment | one line per gap: picture, route, length, images, cost | the `video` route's length cap becomes 30 s a take; the cost line uses Wan's price | **small change** |
| 3 Detailed plan | world, elements, scenes, shots, beats, checks | **as is.** The shot is still the unit; a shot longer than 10 s no longer has to be split; `end_state_exact` stays a plan fact (it still drives the take check) | **as is**, one limit raised |
| 4 Picture search | the round, step 1 (footage frames) and step 2 (sets) | **as is** | **as is** |
| 5 Generated references, sheets, golden pictures | sets for every element: a person's three pictures, a thing's two to four | **as is, plus the fourth reference: one environment picture per scene** (section 3) | **one addition** |
| 6 Shot pictures and video | for a `video` shot: bind → packet → **start image** (→ end image when exact) → prepare-take → take | for a `video` shot: bind → packet → **prepare-take → take**. The start and end images are **not needed** on Wan: the references go in by name, the environment picture carries the layout and light, the take text carries the action by the second | **the start/end image step leaves the video route** (kept for Gemini Omni and for stills) |
| 7 Checks after a take, review, record | drift measured against the start image (locked camera), Gemini Flash reads the take against the plan; the operator reviews; `visuals record` | the take check reads the take against the plan as now; the drift measure compares the first second of the take with the **environment picture** instead of a start image, warning only | **as is**, one input swapped |
| 8 Brand | | **as is** | **as is** |

The routes (`footage, still, graphic_sequence, video, drawn`) stay five. Wan serves only `video`. HyperFrames (`drawn`), stills and graphic sequences are untouched.

### 2a. What is no longer needed on the video route (old ways, design/35 rule)

| Gone from the `video` route on Wan | Why | Kept where |
|---|---|---|
| the start keyframe and the end keyframe of a video shot (`image --kind keyframe`, `end_prompt`, `end_region`) | Wan takes references, not frames; the environment picture + the sets + the text do their work | kept for Gemini Omni takes (the `google` generator) and for every `still` shot; `frame2video` is the Wan twin of it, offered only if the test round finds a shot type that needs it |
| the start/end pair checks: alignment, the pair difference check, "two shots never share a start", the not-yet gate on a start image, Image1-is-the-golden rule | they check the start image | kept on `google` |
| the single-shot sentence, `<FIRST_FRAME>` tags, "Use Image1 as the starting frame" (`motion_text`) | Wan's house shape is `@ImageN` references and the timeline | kept on `google` |
| the 10 s cap and the split rule (OMNI_ONLY) | Wan allows 30 s | kept on `google` |
| the take-drift measure against the start image | no start image | the same measure against the environment picture, warning only |

Retired by name on the Wan route: none yet. Nothing is deleted; the generator choice per shot (`vw visuals generator --provider … --model …`, design/124) picks the route, Wan becomes the **default** for new films once the test round passes, Gemini Omni stays in the catalogue.

### 2b. What is new

1. **The environment reference** (section 3).
2. **The Wan take text** (`wan_text`): the same record as today's take (`videowizard-video-request-v1`: motion prompt, subject reconciliation, continuity, timeline by the second, ambient, light, person lines, heading, gaze, tool lines, fixed line, camera line) written in Wan's shape: the references first ("the mason (@Image1), his face (@Image2), the trowel and pan (@Image3), the place (@Image4)"), the place from the scene's layout and light, the persons' fixed look, then the beats with their seconds, the camera line, "no dialogue, no captions". No counts of identical things (design/165). No frame tags.
3. **The Wan provider** (`video_providers.WanProvider`): runs the CLI on the VM as the worker's user: `--dry-run` first (every `@ImageN` must resolve, the settings must match the record), then submit, then `result get` on each poll (the worker's existing submit-once / poll-with-backoff / retain loop, `process_take`, unchanged), then the saved MP4 into storage. Credits before and after are the cost record. Errors mapped: 4018 stop; 4007/50000/100101 wait; 50001 credits; 9005–9013 input; 9007/9008/9012/10017 content; 9001 or HTML service.
4. **The catalogue entry**: `{'provider': 'wan', 'model': 'wan3.0', 'min_seconds': 2, 'max_seconds': 30, 'duration_control': 'explicit or smart', 'end_image': False}`; the shot's generator choice selects it; the film record's shape sets `--ratio`.
5. **Inputs the record carries for later films** (designed now, no page for them yet): `audio` (up to five MP3/WAV clips with ranges: a line of dialogue, a music bed), `videos` (up to five clips with ranges: a footage stretch as a likeness or a continuation), `audio_output` (false for a narrated documentary, true where the film wants the generated sound), `smart_duration`. A documentary sends none of the first two and `audio_output=false`.
6. **Keys**: `VW2_WAN_ACCESS_KEY` in `server/.env` on the VM (never in git, never in chat; SK places it as a file, as the Gemini key was); the worker exports it as `WAN_ACCESS_KEY` for the CLI's process only. Node 22+ and `@wan-ai/cli` installed on the VM under the service user; the install's skill sync is irrelevant there.
7. **Concurrency**: the worker keeps at most 8 Wan takes in flight (one shared account); a 4007/50000/100101 answer is a wait, not a failure.

### 2c. The Wan take text and the tagging (SK, 5 Nov: "have you considered that")

The server writes the whole text (`wan_text`), in this order; the assistant supplies only the motion prompt and continuity, as today:

1. **The references by name, in the order the files are passed**: "the mason (@Image1); his face (@Image2); the helper (@Image3); the pointing trowel and the iron mortar pan (@Image4); the place, this scene with nobody in it (@Image5)". The list is built from the shot's bindings, so a number can never drift from a file; `--dry-run` before every submit confirms each tag resolved, else the take is refused before any credit is spent.
2. **One line per reference on what to take from it**: identity, hair and clothes from a person's picture; surface, colour and wear from a thing's; layout, light and air from the place's. Where a photograph's pose must not carry over, the line frees it ("his hands are empty; nothing on his head").
3. **The place**, one sentence from the scene's layout, light and air.
4. **Fixed positions and facing** against the landmarks: today's heading, gaze, fixed and tool lines, unchanged.
5. **The beats with their seconds** from the plan's timeline (design/128). With smart duration, the seconds stay and the last line reads "then the take ends".
6. **The camera**: one line, or one line per cut when the shot has angles inside it, each with its second.
7. **The closing rules**: no counts of identical things (design/165), no camera equipment named, no dialogue, no captions.

Gone from the Wan text: the `<FIRST_FRAME>` tags, "Use Image1 as the starting frame", the single-shot sentence. The person's working-pose picture (set picture 2) is sent only when the shot wants that pose; the full-length neutral picture and the face go by default.

## 3. The fourth reference: the environment (SK's question "is it already implemented?")

**Partly, not as asked.** Today a scene's surroundings reach the generator three ways:
1. A `place` element (kind `place` in the plan) gets a **set** like any thing: two to four pictures of the place from the kept prompt. That is per *element*, one for the whole film ("the house plot"), not per scene.
2. The footage frames approved for the place are bound to a shot as the **look reference** ("take only its light and colour", design/104 D2).
3. The **start keyframe** composes the scene's layout, landmarks and light for one shot. It leaves the Wan route (section 2a), so this way is gone there.

**Missing:** one picture per **scene** of the scene as the plan describes it, **nobody in it**: the layout, the landmarks in their places, the light, the air, the time of day, the place's signs of use. That is what SK wants tagged in every Wan prompt as "the place (@ImageN)". So it is built:

- **Reference kind `environment`** (added to `REF_KINDS`), `element_id` = the scene's place element (or none when the scene has no place element), **`scene_id`** on the row, `status` candidate → approved like every reference.
- **Made at step 5**, after the sets are approved, one per scene of every accepted gap on the `video` route, by the same image job as a set (the kept preamble, then a server-written block from `Scene.layout`, `landmarks`, `location_time`, `light`, `air`, the place's `signs_of_use`; the approved footage frames of the place and the place's set as parents; "no person, no animal, no text; the camera at the shot's eye level"). The server writes the whole prompt; the assistant adds nothing.
- **Judged** on the References step of the page like a set (Yes / No / Correct it with words), one row per scene; the chat tool lists them under `vw visuals status`.
- **Bound automatically** to every shot of its scene at `bind` (no operator step), and sent as the last `@Image`.
- **A scene that `continues_footage`** (design/124-b item 11): the approved footage frame beside the gap is the environment picture; no AI picture is made and nothing is spent.
- **Checks**: `image_check` on the environment picture: no person, the named landmarks present, the light as written (the kept checks, warning only).

Cost: one image per scene (≈ the price of one set picture), on the operator's cost record like a set.

## 4. The test round before the build (SK: "test that out and then finalise")

Run by hand with the CLI, on the VM (the key lives there; the runs are the account's own history anyway), with the four VW-12 scenes of design/169 and the mason scene, reusing the approved pictures. Every run judged on half-second strips at 480 px (design/167 D8). Budget: about 12 runs, roughly 30 credits each at 10 s and 720P; a 30 s run will cost more, measured.

| # | Question | Runs | Pass when |
|---|---|---|---|
| W1 | Timeline by the second (our text) vs **smart duration** (the model ends the take) | the plinth scene (8 s): one with `--duration 8`, one with `--smart-duration=true` | the beats land at their seconds in the first; the second ends at a sensible place and its length is recorded; the operator's cut is not harmed |
| W2 | References by name: person set picture 1 + face + tool set + **environment picture**, no start image | the pointing scene and the string scene | identity held; the environment's layout and light held; nothing from a photograph's pose leaks (the pan-on-the-head lesson) |
| W3 | **30 s single take, director's play**: three angles inside one take, a push-in, a pan, hands at the joint | the mason scene written as one 30 s take with cuts named at their seconds | the cuts come where named; the person and the wall hold across them; the hands do the work |
| W4 | **Counting**: five bricks taken one at a time from a pan to a wall, no input video | one run, 10 s, a count of five in the text, bricks drawn large | the count holds at the end (SK's test says "a little better than Gemini") |
| W5 | **Silent take** and the assembly: `--audio-output=false`, 30 fps into a 24/25 fps film | one run of W2 re-cut through the assembly's fill (`offset_frames`) | no audio track; the fill places and plays at the film's fps |
| W6 | **Environment picture alone**: does the place picture carry the layout better than the place element's set? | the kiln scene twice: once with the environment picture, once with the place set only | the kiln, stacks and clay heap in their places only in the first |

Written up as design/171 with the strips; the pass line per test decides the build's defaults (duration mode, which set pictures go in, whether `frame2video` is ever needed).

## 6. Unstated requirements and edge cases

- A Wan job that the CLI reports ambiguously: `wan task list` before any resubmit (never pay twice); the worker's `submission_unknown` state already means this.
- The CLI's version: `wan update --check` on the worker's start, logged, never updated on its own.
- 4018 (membership lapsed) stops every Wan take with one clear line; the shot keeps its preparation.
- More than 8 takes queued: they wait in our queue, not at Wan.
- A picture larger than Wan accepts (`LOCAL_FILE_VALIDATION_FAILED`): the worker scales it down with ffmpeg into the spool, never in storage.
- A scene with no landmarks or no place element: the environment picture is still made from `layout` and `light`; with neither, refused by name at the plan check.
- An older film (before this change) keeps its start images and Gemini Omni; nothing is migrated.
- The take text's length: well under 20,000 characters; the server refuses beyond it.
- Audio inputs: each clip 1–15 s, five at most, 15 s in total; the server refuses more before the CLI does.
- Watermark: Premium gives none; `savedFiles[].watermark` is checked and a watermarked file refused.
- Secrets: the CLI's `auth status` prints the account's e-mail and address; the worker reads only `authenticated` and never logs the rest.
