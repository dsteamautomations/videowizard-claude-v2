The place: a house site on red earth in rural Tamil Nadu, mid-morning, dry; warm sun from the upper left, hard, short shadows to the right. The wall runs from the lower left of the frame away to the right background at chest height, its top course open with the cavities showing; a stack of red bricks at the wall's near end on the mason's right, the mortar pan on the plinth beside it. A neem tree behind. Only these two people in the frame.

This is one film sequence of thirty seconds made of three shots, cut exactly at the seconds given. Within each shot the camera does what its line says and nothing else.

Shot 1, [0-10 s], wide, from the near end of the wall at eye level, the camera locked off. The mason stands on the near side of the wall at frame centre, facing the wall, his left side to the camera. At the start a brick lies on the wall top in front of him, on edge, not yet bedded, and the trowel is in his right hand.
[0-3 s] He spreads mortar from the trowel along the top of the course to his right of the brick.
[3-6 s] He takes a brick from the stack with his left hand, sets it on edge on the mortar beside the first, and taps it down with the trowel's handle.
[6-10 s] He takes a second brick from the stack, sets it on edge a hand's width further along, leaving an open cavity between it and the one before, and taps it down. The helper walks in from the right background along the far side of the wall, carrying nothing, and stops across the wall from the mason.

Shot 2, [10-20 s], close, from the mason's right side, the camera at the height of the wall top, slowly pushing in toward his hands. Only his hands, forearms and the trowel in frame with the bricks on edge.
[10-14 s] The trowel scrapes the squeezed-out mortar off the brick face and flicks it back into the pan.
[14-18 s] The trowel's tip presses the mortar into the vertical joint between the two bricks and strikes it flush.
[18-20 s] His left hand rests on the brick top; the trowel lifts off; the joint is flush and neat.

Shot 3, [20-30 s], wide from the far side of the wall, looking back at the mason over the wall top, the helper in the right foreground with his back three-quarter to the camera; the camera pans slowly left to follow the helper.
[20-24 s] The helper bends to the stack on his side of the wall, lifts two bricks in his two hands and straightens.
[24-28 s] He walks left along the wall with the two bricks and sets them on the wall top within the mason's reach; the mason nods once.
[28-30 s] The helper steps back empty-handed; the mason picks one of the two bricks up and turns it on edge; the shot ends.
