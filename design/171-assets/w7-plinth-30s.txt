The place: a house site on red earth in rural Tamil Nadu, morning, dry. The low fresh plinth at knee height with a grey cement top, dusty with sand and grit; the neat stack of wire-cut bricks behind its right end; the coconut tree at the left beyond it; the bucket with the mug on the red earth at the plinth's foot near the middle; a wooden peg driven into the earth just beyond the plinth's near-right corner with a thin white cotton line tied to a far peg and lying slack along the plinth's edge. Morning sun from the left of frame, moderately hard, neutral-warm daylight, short shadows to the right; a light breeze in the coconut fronds. Only one person in the frame, always this worker.

This is one film sequence of thirty seconds made of three shots, cut exactly at the seconds given. Within each shot the camera does what its line says and nothing else.

Shot 1, [0-10 s], wide, the camera locked off a little above eye level looking slightly down, the plinth across the lower half of the frame, the worker full figure. At the start he stands at the near side of the plinth's left end, his left side toward the camera, bent forward, the broom in his right hand with its tip on the plinth top.
[0-6 s] He sweeps the plinth top in short strokes away from his body, stepping to the right along the plinth as he goes; sand and grit fall off the far edge and a little red dust rises and settles; the swept stretch goes from dusty to clean.
[6-8 s] He lays the broom on the red earth at the plinth's foot and takes the mug out of the bucket.
[8-10 s] He throws the first mugful of water along the swept top in a flat arc; the top darkens where it lands.

Shot 2, [10-20 s], close, the camera locked off at the height of the plinth top, a little to the worker's right, looking along the top: the wet grey top fills the lower half of the frame, his hands and the mug enter from the right.
[10-14 s] The mug dips into the bucket at the frame's lower right and comes up full; the hand throws the water along the top in a flat sheet; the water spreads and the top darkens.
[14-18 s] A second mugful follows the same way; the wet patch joins the first; drops run off the far edge.
[18-20 s] The hand lowers the empty mug out of frame; the top lies dark and wet, steaming faintly in the sun.

Shot 3, [20-30 s], low, the camera locked off at knee height at the plinth's near-right corner looking along the plinth's edge toward the far end, the peg at the lower right, the line running away up the frame to the left. At the start only the worker's hands and forearms are in frame at the lower right: the left hand on the head of the peg, the right hand holding the free end of the line; the line lies slack on the earth all the way to the far peg.
[20-25 s] The right hand draws the line back toward the near peg; the slack lifts off the earth and the line straightens until it runs taut and dead straight along the plinth's top edge.
[25-29 s] The right hand winds the taut line twice round the near peg and tucks the end under itself, then plucks the line once; it quivers and settles straight.
[29-30 s] The right hand lifts off; the line stays taut along the edge.
