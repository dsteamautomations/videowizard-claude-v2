The place: a chimney brick kiln on a dry plain in rural Tamil Nadu, morning, dry. The tall tapering brick chimney with grey-white smoke drawing steadily from its top and drifting toward frame right; the long earth-banked firing trench at its foot; long rows of green bricks drying on the bare earth; a waist-high stack of bricks at the near end of the rows; the earth path with wheel ruts; a flat plain with a few trees behind. Morning sun from frame left, moderately hard, neutral daylight; the smoke softens the sky behind the chimney; a faint heat shimmer over the trench. The two workers are the only people.

This is one film sequence of thirty seconds made of three shots, cut exactly at the seconds given. Within each shot the camera does what its line says and nothing else.

Shot 1, [0-10 s], wide, the camera locked off at standing eye level on the path: the chimney in the left third, the two workers right of centre at the near end of the drying rows beside the stack, three-quarter toward the camera. At the start the first worker, in the orange vest, is bent at the waist with both hands on a pair of dried green bricks in the nearest row; the second, in the purple shirt, stands upright at the stack, hands free.
[0-4 s] The first worker lifts the pair of bricks from the row and straightens.
[4-7 s] He hands the pair across to the second worker at waist height; the second takes them in both hands.
[7-10 s] The second worker sets the pair on top of the stack and lets go; the first bends to the row again.

Shot 2, [10-20 s], close, the camera locked off at waist height beside the stack, looking at the two men's hands and the top of the stack; the chimney soft in the background at the left.
[10-14 s] The first worker's hands lift a pair of green bricks into frame from below and pass them to the right.
[14-17 s] The second worker's hands take the pair and set them on top of the stack, squaring them against the bricks already there.
[17-20 s] Both pairs of hands leave the frame; the stack top holds the new bricks; the smoke drifts behind.

Shot 3, [20-30 s], wide from the path further along, the camera at standing eye level panning slowly left along the drying rows toward the chimney.
[20-26 s] The pan travels across the long rows of green bricks, the trench and the heat shimmer, the two workers small at the stack continuing their work in the middle distance.
[26-30 s] The pan settles with the chimney and its smoke at the left of the frame and the workers at the right; the shot holds there.
