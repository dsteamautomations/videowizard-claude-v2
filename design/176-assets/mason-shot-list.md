# Mason scene, 10 s, three shots (SK's brief, 5 Nov)

The place: an open construction site in Tamil Nadu, dry grey-brown ground, midday sun. A plinth wall of red clay bricks, three courses high, runs across the frame from left to right at the back. The mason works at the wall on the RIGHT of the frame, facing the wall. On the ground to the mason's LEFT (screen-left of him, in every shot) is a pile of red bricks, stacked. Beside the mason, a square board with grey mortar. The helper comes from the LEFT.

Fixed order from left to right in every shot: helper → brick pile → mason → wall behind. The camera never crosses to the other side of the wall, so this order never flips.

| Shot | Time | Camera | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–3.5 s | Behind the helper, handheld, following him with a slight shake, low | The helper walks away from the camera towards the mason, the steel pan of bricks on his head, both hands steadying it. Ahead: the wall, the mason bending at the wall on the right with the yellow-handled trowel in his right hand, the brick pile on the ground to the mason's left. |
| 2 | 3.5–6 s | Side-on medium shot, camera low, from the front-right | The helper squats and lowers the pan to the ground next to the brick pile. The mason, on the right of the frame, scrapes mortar from the board with the yellow-handled trowel and spreads it on the top course of the wall. |
| 3 | 6–10 s | Close low shot on the pile | The helper's hands lift bricks from the pan, two at a time, and set them on the pile, three times. The pan stays on the ground in front of the pile. At the right edge the mason's trowel with the yellow handle spreads mortar on the wall. |

Things the frames must show: the steel pan (silver, round, shallow), red bricks (in the pan, on the pile, in the wall), the trowel with the yellow handle, the mortar board (grey heap on brown board), the wall courses, the helper's green lungi and white vest, the mason's blue shirt and checked lungi, the towel on the mason's shoulder.
