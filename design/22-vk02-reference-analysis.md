# 22 — What the finished rat-trap bond film (VK02) shows about the edit

Date 15 Oct 2026. Source: `VK02 - Rat Trap Bond.mp4` (SK's finished film, 3840x2160, 24 fps, 5 min 6 s). Analysed locally, no paid AI.

## Method
- Every half second of VK02 was compared with every half second of the 46 VW-2 clips. The comparison uses the centre of the picture only, which avoids the titles, subtitles and logos.
- Scores split cleanly: 0.3 to 0.6 means no match, 0.8 to 1.0 means the same footage. The threshold used is 0.75.
- "Other" means not from the 46 VW-2 clips: AI video, graphics and infographics, the title and end card, split-screen composites, and possibly footage outside VW-2's set. Contact sheets were checked by eye to tell these apart.

Each is a different moment of the same clip, used about a minute or more apart.

## Observations
1. **Real shots are short.** Typically 2 to 3.5 s, cut in quick runs: 3:21 to 3:52 is ten real shots in 31 s. VW-2 options v1 held some shots for up to 9 s (for example, scene 5's first shot).
2. **AI and graphic stretches are long.** Typically 4 to 25 s, and every one is under 30 s.
3. **Some abstract lines use real footage plus a text title.** Examples: "Saves Raw Materials" at 0:58 is on C0131; "Lowers Building Expenditure" at 1:30 is on C0085.
4. **Nearly all the footage was used** (40 of 46 clips), each mostly once.
5. **A finishing layer sits on top of the edit:** orange lower-third titles on most shots, burned-in subtitles, a mascot, logos, a cyan frame, infographics such as the storey-strength cards, and an end card.

## Conclusions beyond what SK stated (to confirm with SK before any rule changes)
- **C1 Shot length.** Should real shots be cut short, about 2 to 4 s, in runs, rather than held long? This would be a new rule. Today VW-2 sets only a minimum hold.
- **C2 Abstract lines.** Should an abstract line be allowed real footage that relates to it, with a text title carrying the message, as at 0:58 and 1:30? Or should abstract lines stay AI by default, as agreed in design/21?
- **C3 Reuse.** The finished film reuses three clips at distant points. Under design/21 these would be offered as decision slides. That matches what was done.
- **C4 Finishing layer.** Should VW-2 plan the lower-third titles and infographics, for example by proposing title text per shot? Or do they stay in Resolve finishing, out of VW-2's scope?
- **C5 The 3 s minimum** applies to AI shots only. Real shots of about 2 s are normal in the finished film.

## Limits
- The matching only finds footage from the 46 VW-2 clips. Some "other" stretches look like real footage from outside that set (for example, sweeping at 3:10 and curing with a hose at 4:20). These were counted as other.
- The real-versus-AI split inside "other" was judged by eye from contact sheets.
