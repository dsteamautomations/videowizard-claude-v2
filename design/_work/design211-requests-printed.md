# design/211: the requests of the reference sets, printed

Built on 17 Nov 2026 with the real function `ref_sets.set_request`, after the review of the three critics. Nothing was sent to any picture model.

## What is real and what is invented

- Real: the things of VW-24 (name, kind, description, requirements, views, sizes, material spec), from `ux1-mockup/vw24-part1/evidence.json`; the footage frames named as inputs are VW-24's own approved frames (the pan, the tile, the concrete, the pouring).
- Invented, because that file lacks it: every scene and shot (the place and time of each thing, the garments of the two people); the look of the film; the view and the verdict of each footage frame (VW-24 recorded no look; the pan's is marked weak here to show that sentence); one operator's correction (the trowel) and one line added by the writer (the fan regulator), to show the order of authority.
- Invented things of other kinds of film: a perfume bottle with a label (an advertisement) and a software step with no hands (a product film).

How a set is asked: three turns of one conversation. Turn 1 is the whole request with the first picture's view; turns 2 and 3 name the next view. The check lines are asked of each picture by the program's check; all are "must".
