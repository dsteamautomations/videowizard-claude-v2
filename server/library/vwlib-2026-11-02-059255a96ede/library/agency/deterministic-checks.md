# Vague words into visible facts

A picture model draws what it can see in the words. A vague word gives it nothing to draw, so it falls back on its own glossy default. Rewrite every vague word from the brief into facts a person could check by looking at the picture, and list the rewrites in the answer form.

## General: every category

### Write instead

| Vague word in the brief | Write instead (visible facts, chosen for this shot) |
| --- | --- |
| stunning, beautiful, epic, masterpiece, best quality, 8K, ultra-detailed, hyper-realistic, flawless, perfect | Delete it. If something is meant, name one fact: the light ("overcast daylight"), a surface ("chipped paint"), a material ("brushed aluminium"). |
| premium, luxury, high-end, big-brand | The materials and their finish, how many objects, how much plain space around the subject, the light's direction and softness. |
| cinematic, filmic, dramatic | The light's direction and contrast, what is near, middle and far, the lens feel ("long lens, shallow depth"), the colour of the light. |
| Instagram, influencer, social or UGC style | The capture as a fact ("a phone photo at eye level"), the setting, the light source, the objects named. |
| studio shot, commercial look, professional | The background's colour and material, where the main light sits and how soft it is, the shadow it casts. |
| like a brand, a film or an artist; similar to X; X style | The traits taken from X, written as facts: layout, materials, light, colour, lettering. Never the name. |
| in the style of the reference picture | Which one thing the reference gives (layout, light, colour, texture or medium); describe the subject separately. |
| centre, middle, left third | A landmark and a distance in a unit the picture shows (bricks, paces, a hand, a doorway). |
| modern, clean, minimal | The number of elements, the background, the margin, the line and surface qualities. |

### What every picture prompt says

Where the subject sits (a landmark and a distance); where the light comes from and how soft it is; the main materials or surfaces; and the words to be read in the picture, only through `exact_text`, or none. A prompt without these is rewritten before it is sent. These are facts, not keywords: a list of words without a picture behind them does not count.

### Anything built or real

Anything built (a sign, a stage, a shelf, a wall, a bridge) looks physically buildable: no floating or impossible parts. When a picture shows a real place, its walls, doors, windows and perspective stay as they are.

## For advertisement-product

The product is large and unobstructed. Its shape, packaging, label and identity never change. Never add a claim about what it does, a price or a certification that the client did not give.
