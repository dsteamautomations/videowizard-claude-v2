# Video takes playbook

A take is one video clip made from an approved start image (and an approved end image only when the shot's final state must be exact). Read with `RULEBOOK.md`, `video/craft/camera-lighting-vocabulary.md` and the video model's sheet. The server writes the picture tags, the camera line, the single-scene line, the length, the timeline of the plan's beats and the end state around your words; you write only the motion brief.

## General: every category

### Decisions already taken

| Decision | Value | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frame first | A take that shows a real product, place or recurring person starts from an approved picture, never from words alone. | Words alone invent labels, faces and places. |
| The brief says only what moves | The start image already holds identity, composition and text. | Re-describing the picture invites the model to redraw it. |
| One shot, one move | One unbroken shot; at most the one camera move the shot names, otherwise a steady camera. | The model adds cuts and drift when it is not held. |
| Motion hold is checked | After the take, the person's traits, any label, the palette and any text are compared with the start image. A broken take is made again from the image, never patched. | A drifted take does not match the film around it. |

### The motion brief

1. **The subject's action:** one physical action and its visible consequence ("lifts the brick; mortar squeezes out at the joint").
2. **Around it:** one or two things that move on their own (steam, dust, fabric, leaves, a passing figure in the background).
3. **What stays:** the things that must not change (the label, the person's traits, the object in hand).

Bodies, materials and consequences render; "cinematic", "dynamic", "beautiful" and named feelings do not. Lead with the subject and its action. Describe consequences, not only actions: the lid actually unscrews; the dust settles. Never pair contradictions ("still pond" with "flowing water"). Do not describe what the start image already shows.

### Checklist lines

- One scene, no cuts. must.
- The person's traits, any label, the colours and any text in frame hold through the whole take against the start image. must.
- The named camera move happened and nothing else; with no move named, the camera stays still. must.
- No sound, captions or dialogue appear. must.
- Nothing appears from nowhere or vanishes; nothing ends in an impossible place. must.
- Each action's consequence is visible. should.
- The start image is recognisable as the take's first frame. must.

### What goes wrong, and the fix

| Habit | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| A label or small text softens in motion | Slow the move, or make the take again from the image |
| The motion is ignored in a short take | Give the action a speed and a visible result; a take toward 5 s or more moves more |
| The person or object drifts | Name what stays (step 3 of the brief); make it again from the image |

## For advertisement-product

- The product never deforms; motion belongs to hands or surroundings; the label stays sharp and readable throughout.

## For training-education and documentary-explainer

- The working action is shown whole and at a speed a viewer can follow: the tool, the hand, the result.

## Not available yet (filed for later categories)

VideoWizard makes silent, steady, single-shot takes today. If sound, handheld cameras or dialogue are ever allowed:
- **Sound (advertisement-product, social-ugc, narrative-story):** room tone and one sound, or music by kind; dialogue short, attributed to a visible action, in quotes.
- **Handheld phone style (social-ugc):** "shot on a smartphone, handheld, vertical", natural light, direct address; the person fictional or consenting, an AI testimonial disclosed.
